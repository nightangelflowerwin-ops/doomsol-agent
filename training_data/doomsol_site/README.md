# DoomSol maps and instructions snapshot

Source: <https://doomsol.io/>

Downloaded: 2026-08-31

`doomsol-maps-and-instructions-2026-08-31.zip` contains:

- 38 map PNG images published by the updated site
- `maps.json` with map names, chapters, descriptions, trivia, credits, and references
- `instructions.md` with the map information in readable Markdown
- `site-content.json` with the complete structured site dataset
- `download-manifest.json` with source URLs, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes

These assets are reference material and labels. They are not live collision
geometry or proof of the agent's position during a DoomSol gameplay session.

The site states that level descriptions and trivia are adapted from Doom Wiki
under CC BY-SA 4.0. Original DOOM maps and designs remain the property of their
respective rights holders; consult the source site and included references.
